General Information

Skin cancer occurs when abnormal skin cells grow uncontrollably. Changes in a skin lesion should be monitored carefully.

Things to observe

  • Changes in size or shape
  • Changes in colour
  • Irregular borders
  • Persistent itching or discomfort
  • Bleeding or unusual skin changes
⚠️ This AI result is not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for further evaluation.
🛡️

Preventive Measures

Protecting your skin and monitoring skin changes can help maintain good skin health.

Recommended measures

  • Protect your skin from excessive sun exposure
  • Use sunscreen as appropriate
  • Wear protective clothing in strong sunlight
  • Monitor existing skin lesions
  • Pay attention to new or unusual changes
  • Consult a healthcare professional when needed
💡 Regular skin observation and professional evaluation are important.
